# Almanac Study — Editorial series and communication design v1
**Created:** 22 Nov 2026. **Status:** proposed implementation under Campaign Mode PR #5, not owner-approved or published.
**Doctrine:** Learning that responds. Campaigns are a genuine **first act of learning**, not a billboard.

## The public promise
Every educational post must do at least one of these: help learners understand a concept, invite a meaningful recall/application attempt, show what they missed and how to improve, or help select the next purposeful learning step. Product descriptions and inspirational posts should be intentional, not overwhelm useful teaching.

## Series architecture
| Series | Role | Native formats | Recurrence starting hypothesis | Primary learner action | Commercial pathway |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ONE IDEA, CLEARER | Concept comprehension | 20–45s reel, comparison carousel | 1–2/week | Explain or save a distinction | Ask for relevant course/topic resource |
| THINK BEFORE YOU TURN | Original practice with reasoned feedback | question card + explanation post; Stories | ~1/week | Predict, justify, correct | Sample of practice-based learning |
| THE STUDY DESK | Useful reflection and original quotes | editorial quote, simple 3-step visual | ~1/week | Apply a specific learning habit | Build trust and return behavior |
| INSIDE ALMANAC | Demonstrate authentic learning media | actual redacted slides, video excerpt, approved flashcard/audio examples | only when reviewed assets available | Understand how formats work together | Qualified resource inquiry |
| YOUR NEXT STEP | Listen and personalize the next content | poll, Story prompt, exam-need question | ~1/week | Name one study bottleneck voluntarily | Qualify needs and shape new offers |
| THE RETURN VISIT | Feedback and progress | explanation follow-up, weekly recap | anchored to preceding learning post | Recall and revisit a missed concept | Repeat use and sustained engagement |

## Communication hierarchy
1. **Teach:** one standalone learning outcome. Do not hold back the entire answer behind email.
2. **Invite:** one optional step: save, attempt, revisit, respond, or request a verified resource.
3. **Respond:** provide a genuinely helpful next explanation, a clarified path, or an honest availability response.
4. **Learn:** aggregate unmet topic demands and recurring misconceptions (without identifying learners) to influence the next resource.
5. **Serve:** if there is a verified product, clear rights and an approved payment route, explain scope and price truthfully.

## Distinctive visual language (candidate)
Anchor to the actual post: ivory/off-white field; electric cobalt-blue small logo linework; deep navy headline; restrained coral dot or underline. Keep calm whitespace and readable 1080×1350 portraits. Avoid heavy stock photography, generic graduation props, exaggerated neon, motivational wallpaper, 'score guarantees' and school logos. These approximate colors are directions, not locked production tokens.
- **ONE IDEA, CLEARER:** split-screen paired concepts, large type and minimal source-appropriate line diagram. Each slide makes sense alone.
- **THINK BEFORE YOU TURN:** bold single problem, answer spaces; separate next card contains full rationale and why alternatives fail.
- **THE STUDY DESK:** typographic poster with one original quote; a practical habit in the caption makes it useful.
- **INSIDE ALMANAC:** documentary screenshots/excerpts of genuine reviewed outputs; cite the source inventory internally.
- **YOUR NEXT STEP:** uncluttered single-question card with short choices and accessible contrast.
- **THE RETURN VISIT:** clean 'you may have missed' correction card showing why, not clickbait.

## The social call-to-action system
CTA types: (A) learning behavior, (B) voluntary feedback, (C) resource inquiry. ONE main CTA per post. The posted address for course inquiries is `Almanacstudy.support@gmail.com`; do not claim a paid full NR 601 video is immediately deliverable until verified. Never advertise payment instructions/checkout while route eligibility is unverified.

## Measures
Distribution: age-matched reach/views, shares, saves. Learning proxy: substantial attempts/reasoning in comments, correction discussion, return visits if the platform can actually measure. Customer discovery: qualified subject/exam inquiries by source, reply speed, verified resource interest, permitted sale and retention later. Unknowns are blank, never zero.

## Editorial quality checks
- Actual benefit in the free post; no forced email address merely for basic explanations.
- Clinical/medical education content requires verified source, clinical/item review, rights review.
- Never copy student test items or lift a question bank from third-party repositories.
- Quote authorship is Almanac original unless accurately sourced. No fake endorsements.
- No promises of adaptive AI tutoring, custom course readiness, school partnerships, exam guarantees.
- Distinguish NR 601 user-reported original pack from independently inspected and licensed commercial materials.

## First 14-day hypothesis
Start with a balanced, reviewable mix, not 'daily promotional posting'. Minimum useful first sequence: method carousel -> original editorial quote -> audience need prompt -> authentic study-pack demonstration when assets reviewed -> original practice and answer follow-up after subject review. Assess 72h cohorts first; change one creative variable at a time. Do not impose a compulsory post schedule without editorial capacity and owner approval.
