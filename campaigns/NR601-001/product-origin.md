## Campaign-selection rule, v1.1
Before creating *any* follow-up post, examine the **four-topic map** and eligible asset library:
- For each topic: official title/objectives, source IDs, vetted concepts, ready assets by format, clarity/accuracy risk, copyright status, audience benefit, and a natural CTA.
- Prefer demonstrations showing the breadth and quality of preparation: structured topic map, explanations, spaced recall, original questions with rationales, audio/video review, and practical study plan.
- Use brief topic-specific excerpts for standalone teaching and evidence of utility. Do not use more Katz/Lawton material merely because the first post mentioned it.
- Distinguish the learner's personal study pack and any commercially deliverable Almanac product; do not assume the former has been licensed or approved as the latter.
- Keep NR 601 distinct from other project courses such as **NUR-635 Advanced Pharmacology**; do not import their topic labels, products or assets.
