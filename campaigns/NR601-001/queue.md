## P03 — Quiz brief for educational review
**Card 1:** A fictional older adult manages personal bathing and dressing without help but needs assistance with shopping and managing medications. Which category of daily function most directly includes the activities needing help?
(A) Basic activities of daily living (B) Instrumental activities of daily living (C) Neurologic reflexes (D) Vital signs.

**Card 2, never released without review:** Proposed answer B; rationale: shopping and medication management are instrumental daily activities, while bathing/dressing are basic activities. This is a general educational practice item and does not assess the individual's full function or substitute for clinical evaluation.

**Draft caption:** "Which option would you choose—and why? We care about the reasoning more than the letter. Answer below; we'll publish the explanation after review." Subject review required for stem, distractors and rationale.
