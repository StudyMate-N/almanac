## Primary sources consulted
1. Hartford Institute for Geriatric Nursing — [Katz Index of Independence in ADL](https://hign.org/consultgeri/try-this-series/katz-index-independence-activities-daily-living-adl). Describes six core basic ADL functions: bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, continence and feeding. Explains limited sensitivity to small changes.
2. Hartford Institute for Geriatric Nursing — [Lawton IADL Scale](https://hign.org/consultgeri/try-this-series/lawton-instrumental-activities-daily-living-iadl-scale). Describes eight more complex independent-living domains, caveats of self-report, and current recommendation to assess all eight domains regardless of gender. Notes historical gender-based scoring.
3. [OpenStax Fundamentals of Nursing, 22.3](https://openstax.org/books/fundamentals-nursing/pages/22-3-activities-of-daily-living-adls). Independently summarizes Katz's six items and Lawton's eight. OpenStax material should still be credited if relied upon; draft our own original teaching rather than copy pages/tables.
4. [Pérès et al., 2008 — prospective IADL study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18028344/), for the restricted context that complex IADL difficulties may be seen before dementia diagnosis. **Not evidence that Lawton/IADL 'always declines first' for every patient or condition.**
5. [Functional autonomy meta-analysis (2021)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33738665/), noting that both IADL and subtler BADL decline can occur in mild cognitive impairment; avoid absolute claims.

## Check against proposed P02
- **Basic vs instrumental:** well-supported educational distinction.
- **Katz six items:** proposed examples agree with Hartford.
- **Lawton eight items:** proposed categories agree with Hartford/OpenStax.
- **All-gender assessment nuance:** if describing scoring, avoid reproducing historical sex-specific scoring as a current universal recommendation.
- **Published hook 'Know which one slips first':** **clarify context before follow-ups**. In some cognitive-decline trajectories more complex IADLs can change earlier, but this is not a deterministic order of loss across all older adults. Replace unqualified 'Lawton always slips first' with a precise, sourced, condition-scoped explanation if a reviewer approves.
