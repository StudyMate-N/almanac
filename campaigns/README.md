## Campaign operating lanes
| Lane | Purpose | Example | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teach | Provide standalone educational value | Katz vs Lawton comparison | Save/share, trust |
| Practise | Help learners test an understanding | Original mini-question with rationale | Comments, return visit |
| Demonstrate | Show a genuine portion of a reviewable resource | Authorized NR 601 video/page excerpt | Qualified inquiry |
| Connect | Help learners choose their next step | Ask for course/exam subject | Request with consent |
| Retain | Help followers and purchasers keep learning | Correction recap, next-week challenge | Repeat engagement/purchase |

Every item gets one lane, one learner problem, one primary CTA and one measurable hypothesis; avoid five CTAs in one caption. Public resource samples must be genuinely educational and reviewable, not misleading teasers.

## Campaign production cycle
1. **Capture** product truth, existing social post, audience signal and reported metrics.
2. **Prioritize** one course or exam group with actual signal. Nursing NR 601 goes first.
3. **Plan** a sequence around one educational problem, not unrelated daily promotions.
4. **Produce** final copy, video script/storyboard, visual spec, accessibility description, original question/rationale, sources and review checklist.
5. **Review** educational accuracy, source rights, exact product availability, brand fit and CTA/payment mode.
6. **Approve** by an authorized person. Explicit approval is required for publishing.
7. **Publish** manually or via later authorized, supported connection; record link and timestamp.
8. **Observe** metrics at 24h, 72h and 7d where accessible; log inbound request subjects in aggregate without personal email addresses.
9. **Iterate** one variable per experiment, document result and reasoning, then issue the next proposed work item.

## Roles (responsibilities, not claims of hired people)
- Campaign strategist agent: choose hypothesis, calendar, copy, analysis.
- Education editor/reviewer: check syllabus match, question originality, clinical accuracy and educational value.
- Art director: approve recognizable design, mobile readability, captions and accessibility.
- Owner: confirms product availability, offer, permissions, publication, budget and payment collection authority.
- Analyst: aggregates screenshots, lead counts and conversions; flags missing evidence.

## Non-negotiable gates
- No fabricated results, conversion data, testimonials, course/institution affiliation or guaranteed passing outcomes.
- Nursing/clinical content: exact item/version review and credible references before public claims; avoid personal clinical advice.
- No derivative use of copyrighted school assessments, private student data, unauthorized patient stories or other unlicensed resources.
- No auto-sending messages, Facebook posting, advertising spend or checkout changes without approved scope and authority.
- Request-only by default unless a new authorized payment-route review supersedes the October 23 decision; a marketing request is **not** payment authorization.
- Treat quoted prices and delivery timelines as product specifications, and recheck them before publication.

## North-star scorecard
**Primary:** number of qualified inquiries per content cohort; requests that match a real Almanac product; verified paying customers when lawful sales enablement is confirmed; repeat customers.
**Secondary:** 24h/72h/7d post distribution; shares/saves, meaningful comments, subject-tagged email requests, response-to-inquiry time, customer satisfaction/retention where measured.
**Guardrails:** misinformation and copyright incidents, content QA failures, response backlog, refund or complaint signals. There is no numeric success target until a reliable baseline is established.

## Review cadence
- After each post: record publication metadata and 24h/72h/7d measurements if provided.
- Daily campaign pass: check current queue, fresh evidence and blockers; prepare the next draft/recommended experiment. Do not pretend you can fetch private Facebook insights without a connected authorized source.
- Weekly review: compare matched post cohorts, determine which content generated qualified interest, update the experiment log and the skill in a reviewed commit.
- Start with a **14-day learning experiment**, not an assumption that a full marketing calendar is permanently approved.
