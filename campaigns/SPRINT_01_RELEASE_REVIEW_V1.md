## S01 — teach independently (5 slides, 1080×1350)
1. **CAN YOU EXPLAIN WHY?** Subhead: *A quick check after any practice question.*
2. **PREDICT FIRST.** Before looking at the options, write or say what answer you expect. Note why.
3. **EXPLAIN THE CHOICE.** After answering, explain what makes your choice fit—and why a plausible alternative does not.
4. **NAME THE GAP.** If you cannot explain the difference, mark that concept for review. Check the rationale or a reliable source; do not just memorize the letter.
5. **TRY IT ON ONE QUESTION TODAY.** One prediction. One explanation. One next step. *Save this for your next revision session.*

**Caption — proposed:** `Getting the answer right and understanding why are not always the same thing. On your next practice question, predict before you look at the options, explain your choice, and name one thing you still need to review. Start with just one question. Save this routine for your next study session. — Almanac Study`

**Accessibility:** alt text per slide should repeat the teaching content, not simply say “study tips”; provide a high-contrast reading order and avoid placing key information solely in color. Design reviewer checks readable type and safe crop in Facebook feed. No assertion of demonstrated learning improvement or promised exam performance.

**Gate:** owner approves exact caption and artwork version; editorial/design checks; post URL and timestamp captured only after actual publication. No paid CTA or clinical example.

## Evidence ledger and decision rules
**Existing observation:** P01 Facebook screenshot supplied by owner, ~4 hours old on 2026-11-22, showed 360 views and 4 shares. This is a descriptive, age-specific observation, not a controlled baseline for S01 or proof of conversions. Original post URL, exact published timestamp, reach, saves, comments, organic/paid status, subject-tagged inquiries, permitted sales and refunds: **unknown**, not zero.

For each published post, record: `post_id | canonical_url | published_at_with_timezone | format | theme | approved_asset_version | snapshot_at | post_age_hours | views | reach | shares | saves | meaningful_responses | subject_tagged_inquiries | verified_permitted_sales | source_of_each_value | missingness_reason | confounders`. Do not backfill missing values as 0. An observed zero requires explicit source evidence. Capture 24h, 72h, 7d if available, comparing only like-age observations, and identify boosted posts or material distribution differences.

**Pre-registered, not results:** S01 practical-use signal = saves and substantive comments at 72h, with reach/views context; S02 reflection signal = saves/shares at 72h, not treated as equal evidence of learning; S03 discovery signal = non-sensitive categorized replies and distinct subject themes at 72h. No unsupported cross-format causal ranking. Inquiries are only attributed to a post when the source is known; otherwise label unattributed. Permitted sales count only when authorization and transaction evidence exist. Small or missing samples yield `inconclusive`, not `underperforming`.
