## Locked-for-review teaching sequence (NOT locked for publishing)
| Slide | On-art text | Exact accessible description / alt-text draft |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CAN YOU EXPLAIN WHY?** A quick check after any practice question. | Almanac Study introduces a three-step practice routine: predict an answer, explain why it fits, then identify the concept needing review. |
| 2 | **PREDICT FIRST.** Before looking at the options, say or write what answer you expect. Note why. | Step one: Before reading the answer options, predict an answer and give a reason. If the question format does not allow this, make your best prediction before revealing the answer. |
| 3 | **EXPLAIN THE CHOICE.** Explain what makes your answer fit—and why one plausible alternative does not. | Step two: After answering, explain the reasoning for your choice and why a plausible alternative is less suitable. Use the provided rationale or a trustworthy source to check your explanation. |
| 4 | **NAME THE GAP.** If you cannot explain the difference, mark the concept for review. | Step three: When you cannot explain the difference between answers, note the specific concept to revisit. Do not memorize the answer letter alone. |
| 5 | **TRY IT ON ONE QUESTION TODAY.** One prediction. One explanation. One next step. Save this for your next revision session. | Try the routine on one original or legitimately available practice question today: predict, explain, then choose what to revisit. You can save the steps for later. |

**Caption for review:** Getting an answer right and understanding why are not always the same thing. On your next practice question, predict before looking at the options, explain your choice, and name one concept you still need to review. Start with just one question. Save this routine for your next study session. — Almanac Study

**Single primary CTA:** Save this routine for your next revision session. **Learner benefit:** an actionable, self-contained study routine. **Not claimed:** guaranteed retention, exam success, learning-style matching, verified effectiveness or a live adaptive tutor.

## Art direction and export QA
- Five individual portrait slides, **1080 × 1350 px**, in a consistent order; maintain generous crop-safe margins around all instructional copy.
- Editorial Almanac palette: warm ivory base, deep navy type, restrained cobalt and coral accent. The message must remain intelligible without color differentiation.
- Prioritize readable typography and short lines; don't embed tiny diagrams, screenshots of course assessments, invented NotebookLM interfaces or institution marks.
- Every slide should stand on its own with a visible slide index (1/5 etc.); include brand attribution discreetly.
- Verify actual exported pixel dimensions, legibility on a mobile-size preview, text order, typographic consistency and visual contrast; do not mark these verified until real files are inspected.
- Attach the five real image filenames, hashes, final caption, alt descriptions, owner approval and publishing destination to a release manifest before publication.
