# Almanac Study — Sprint 02: Learner-Responsive Campaign Kit
**Status:** Newly produced editorial copy and response system, 22 Nov 2026. Approval required; no posts, emails or DMs sent. Coordinate with PR #7 learning materials; never promise access to unreviewed source media.

## Operating idea
Each free post should teach a precise, independent idea and provide a next step. Where the learner responds, use their expressed study obstacle to prioritize subsequent real topics and formats. The public experience begins to adapt by publishing reviewed answers to verified demand; no false claims of a live AI tutor or official university affiliation.

## P09 — One concept, three ways to learn (media awareness, 5-slide carousel)
**Learning benefit:** helps a student choose an activity by learning need rather than a fixed 'learning style'.
**Slide 1:** `ONE IDEA. MORE THAN ONE WAY TO WORK WITH IT.`
**Slide 2:** `NEED CLARITY?` Read a concise explanation, then explain it back in your own words.
**Slide 3:** `NEED TO REMEMBER?` Cover the answer. Retrieve it from memory. Revisit it later.
**Slide 4:** `NEED TO APPLY?` Try a new problem. Check the explanation—even if you were right.
**Slide 5:** `YOUR NEXT STEP` Which needs attention today: understanding, remembering or applying? Tell us the subject you are preparing for.
**Caption:** `You don't need the same study activity every day. Sometimes you need a clearer explanation. Sometimes you need a memory check. Sometimes you need a new problem to work through. Choose the next activity by what you're trying to learn—not by a label about what kind of learner you are. Which do you need today: understanding, remembering or applying? — Almanac Study.`
**Visual:** editorial ivory and navy, blue category labels, small coral underline, readable 1080x1350 slides; never fill a slide with raw science claims. **Primary CTA:** comment choice. **Status:** copy DRAFT; needs owner design approval.

## P10 — A 20-second learning choice (original 30–40s video / social micro-lesson)
**Learning benefit:** distinguishes passive exposure from a genuine self-check without belittling reading or video.
**Storyboard:**
- 0–5s `You finished the video. What do you remember?` show a page with the last frame fading out.
- 5–12s `Close the notes. Name one idea from memory.` learner pause.
- 12–22s `Explain why it works—or compare it with a different example.`
- 22–32s `If your explanation breaks down, that's the next concept to revisit.`
- 32–40s `Start with one useful question.` End card `ALMANAC STUDY · LEARNING THAT RESPONDS`.
**Voiceover:** `Finishing a video can introduce an idea. To find out whether you can use it, give yourself a quick check. Close your notes and explain one idea from memory. If it's hard to explain, you have found your next review target. Keep it small. One idea. One answer. One next step.`
**Caption:** `Watching, reading and listening can all help you meet an idea. Retrieval helps you check what stayed with you. At the end of your next learning session, try explaining just one concept without looking. What subject are you working on this week?`
**Accessibility:** captions synchronized with original VO, transcript, no unverified screenshots or patient information. **Status:** storyboard/script DRAFT; actual MP4 not created.

## Three micro-response templates — for public comments, not automated messaging
1. **If learner chose a format:** `That can be a useful place to begin. A quick check afterward is to explain one idea without notes. Which topic are you revising?`
2. **If learner names a course not in verified catalog:** `Thanks for sharing the subject. We can review what support is feasible; we don't want to promise a resource before checking its contents and availability.`
3. **If learner asks whether a pack is free or paid:** `Public explanations here are free. Any fuller resource will have its scope, access and cost explained before an order is accepted. Please share the subject you're preparing for if you'd like details.`
**Do not imply human review or same-day availability unless established; don't quote prices from unverified checkout.**
