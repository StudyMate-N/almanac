The loop learns from real evidence and versioned feedback, but does not self-modify its constraints or grant itself additional permissions. Owner retains approval of clinical claims, campaign publication, ads, outreach, product promises and payment operations. If no new evidence exists, report a useful draft or blockers without fabricated gains.
