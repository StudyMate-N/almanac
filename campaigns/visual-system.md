# Almanac Study — Provisional social visual system v1

**Not a final brand redesign.** Start with the already-published Facebook video and logo as recognized public references. The social page currently uses blue wordmark and orange/coral punctuation on a warm cream ground. Historical product UI uses plum/lilac and Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans; don't silently overwrite either system. Future cross-channel token decisions require owner approval.

## Content formats
1. **Study Notes / Comparison:** 1080x1350 portrait; editorial warm cream; blue emphasized labels; orange only as useful accent; strong heading; generous space; original labeled charts/diagrams. Five-slide comparison carousel: problem -> two definitions -> reliable memory cue -> useful recap/next step.
2. **Almanac Challenge:** 1080x1350, strong blue cue at the start, easy-to-read question body, answer options with comfortable spacing and no answer accidentally visible before reveal. Explanation card must carry the rationale and limits.
3. **The Study Desk (quotes):** 1080x1350, restrained typographic rhythm, one genuine memorable line. No unattributed borrowed quote or pseudo-famous attribution. Avoid generic graduation clip art.
4. **Video previews:** 1080x1920 for Reels/Stories; safe-title margins; captions/subtitles; 2–3-second understandable hook; visual progression; specific next step. Never invent a video preview that is not part of an approved or explicitly labeled sample.

## Design constraints
- Type must be legible on an actual phone; do not reduce medical distinctions to tiny footnotes.
- Use authentic content-derived visual hierarchy; no exaggerated urgency, confetti, fake scores or fictitious school logos.
- Visual language must be repeatable across subjects without making all topics identical.
- One core question or teaching point per post; label course-specific content clearly.
- Alt text, contrast and caption quality are part of approval, not postprocessing.

## Attribution and truth
- Footer branding `Almanac Study`, optionally `Independent study resource · not affiliated with a school or program` where useful.
- Use original/licensed illustrations and verifiable references when making external factual statements.
- Product previews must be screenshots of actual reviewed content, not invented product interfaces or mock 'student successes'.
- Keep screenshots of Facebook metrics separate from creative assets; never advertise private analytics as customer testimonials.
