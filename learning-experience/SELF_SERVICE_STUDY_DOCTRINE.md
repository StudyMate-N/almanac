# Almanac — Learner-Governed Self-Service and Self-Study
**Working strategic framework v0.1 | 22 November 2026 | Proposed, not deployed.**

## Central proposition
**Let the learner choose the route. Let Almanac protect the learning standard.**
'Learning that responds' requires two things at once: learner agency over practical study choices, and a trustworthy educational architecture with clear cost, scope, rights, quality, accessibility and assessment boundaries. Self-service is NOT simply offering more checkboxes, and self-study is NOT simply abandoning the learner with a folder of files.

## Two forms of autonomy — keep distinct
**Package-building autonomy (before access):** What subject, goal, scope, timeline, available budget, delivery mode and substantiated extra production does the learner want? Existing request-builder includes package choice, material uploads, beginning preference, delivery mode, timing and bounded add-ons. Do not remove included core formats or double-charge for those.
**Learning-path autonomy (after access):** Where to start, what format to engage first, pace, practice intensity within reasonable constraints, which skill to revisit, whether to accept suggested next steps, and whether to use optional stories, games, audio or musical memory cues. These choices should not require repeated purchases or a new quote unless they require a genuinely new, separately priced production deliverable.

## Four user journeys
1. **Explore openly** — free public lesson, concept comparison, explanatory question, short video/audio; learner can find the next learning step without signing in or surrendering contact details.
2. **Self-study available course/resource** — browse already-reviewed, rights-cleared, released material; choose order and format, build a daily routine, retrieve/receive feedback, track progress. Price/access rights only from actual approved catalog, not by applying custom production price formulas to reused content.
3. **Customize an existing entitlement** — within a purchased/authorized package, choose video-first, read-first, practice-first or balanced; alternate formats; choose target sessions and objective emphasis; change these without losing progress or removing critical content. No charge for preference changes or formats already included in the package.
4. **Request a newly produced course/pack** — bounded selection using existing `OFFERS`, `ENHANCEMENTS`, `SPEEDS`; course-specific inputs + reviewed human production approval. Experimental stories, games and music can be requested as interests but cannot be priced, promised or fulfilled without reviewed scope, capacity and rights. Coaching is a separate explicitly approved service; do not pretend it is included by default.

## Three modes in one learner experience
| Mode | Who determines route? | Almanac's responsibility | Outcome evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explore / self-directed** | learner selects topics/format/order | explain objectives, provide accurate sources and voluntary check | self-initiated practice and reflection |
| **Guided self-study (default)** | learner chooses, system recommends | propose manageable sequence, retrieval checks, feedback, return/review prompts | attempts, explanations, spaced review |
| **Supported study** | learner plus authorized human | investigate persistent gaps and adjust approved resources | specific reviewer or coach intervention |

Mode changes should be reversible. Do not force a beginner to independently manage an unfamiliar subject without scaffolded explanation and attainable suggested defaults. Do not label modes as tiers of intelligence or fixed 'learning styles'.

## A self-study 'recipe' (not a rigid universal formula)
`Goal -> initial check -> review credible explanation -> retrieval -> worked application -> evidence-based feedback -> spaced revisit -> independent transfer`.
Select study-length/pace to suit schedule, with a safe default that can be overridden. The learner may reorder or skip a particular media format, but **cannot omit the required evidence of understanding and still receive a false 'mastered' state**. Reading/video exposure count as engagement; independent retrieval/application counts as evidence. Revisit due concepts using existing FSRS without silently deleting/overriding card histories.

### User-facing adjustments with no added production price
- Start in video, audio, read or practice mode where that media already exists and is authorized for the learner.
- Set a manageable session target (e.g. short, standard or extended), goals/date, optional reminders when consented; never force excessive study.
- Choose quiet/text-only / low-bandwidth mode; text transcripts and captions are accessible, not an add-on.
- Pick themes of weakness, bookmark a concept, request a different explanation already in library; select one of several proposed next steps.
- Add an optional original approved story/game/music cue if available in the entitlement; these are never necessary to demonstrate understanding.
- Change priorities at any time, with clear visibility of what is due and what remains untested.

### Scope or price-changing choices
Only changes that actually require new production labor/resources and have reviewed pricing, measurable scope and support capacity:
- Extra deep-dive video ($35 in observed `learning-offers.ts`, where allowed)
- Additional new simulation ($39, where allowed)
- Additional new complete module ($59, where allowed)
- Speed multiplier on whole prepared package if capacity permits (existing ×1, ×1.4, ×1.9; Complete Course express disabled in validator)
- Experimental interactive story/game/music **NOT CURRENTLY PRICED**. Do not interpret low-fi prototype as included in offers or assign a speculative price.

### Non-negotiable safeguards
- All packages retain their stated core explanations, original practice, necessary feedback, and reviewed version/rights. Do not sell a 'video-only' or 'song-only' pack that removes relevant assessment and accessible alternatives.
- Specific clinical simulations/assessment explanations need exact qualified content review; no copied institution current tests, protected lecture slides, patient identifiers, unlicensed datasets or unsupported personal medical advice.
- Stop self-serve promise if product has no actual reviewed/licensed content, account entitlement, or delivery capacity.
- Never equate a learner's modality choice with a fixed evidence-based learning style; it is a contextual preference that can change.
- All learner data, including study choices, attempts and weakness signals, stays within appropriate permission/retention and is not used by public advertising pixels.
- Learners can override the recommendation and see why the recommendation was made. Clear fallback if data absent.
- The existing request page currently creates an invoice and mentions CAD bank transfer. A later payment review restricted unrestricted public launch. **Do not direct users to pay or represent checkout as approved without a current verified payment-route decision.**
- Explicitly preserve server-side quote revalidation and exact approved catalogue price. No trusting client-calculated totals.

## Self-service shouldn't be a feature farm
Bad: "Choose video, audio, 4 songs, 5 games and 2 slides" with no objective, no evidence checks and no explanation of costs.
Good: "I have a four-topic midterm in two weeks, 30 minutes per day. Start with a short visual explanation, quiz me on topics I miss and let me revisit those using audio while commuting. Show which learning objectives remain untested."
The second request is a personalized experience; the media are its tools, not the product itself.

## Product hierarchy
- **Foundation:** verified learning objectives, approved claims and original evidence-based practice.
- **Interaction:** learner control, optional formats, study sessions, explicit next-step choices.
- **Response:** suggestions from observed performance and selected goals; one clear next step plus alternatives; learner can decline.
- **Commerce:** transparent existing entitlements and cost of *new* approved production; capacity/rights gate.
- **Trust:** reviewer approvals, privacy, accessibility, transparent outcomes and limitations.

## Experiment / falsifiability
First compare **guided choice** with **flat browse** for identical reviewed content and entitlement. Pre-register:
- Can a learner make a reasonable next-step choice without assistance?
- Does a chosen study route yield higher completion of meaningful retrieval or better delayed transfer than an unstructured media list?
- Do accessible alternatives remain equally usable?
- Are choices stable or changing over time? Changing is not a bug.
- Does configurator completion lead to qualified requests without buyer confusion/accidental checkout?
- Can staff fulfill within reviewed cost and capacity?
Report only actual results with comparator caveats; no 'proven adaptive learning' claim before direct evidence.
