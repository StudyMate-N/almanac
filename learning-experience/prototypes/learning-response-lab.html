✦ Almanac Study

Independent low-fi learning prototype · No student data collected

Learning that responds.

Two small activities test what you can explain, not how fast you can click. This is a demonstration of a future learning format, not a live Almanac course feature.

01 / A SHORT STORY

Sam chose the right answer. But why?

Sam answers a practice question correctly but cannot say why another plausible option was wrong. What should Sam do next?

02 / A MINI-GAME

What really checks understanding?

Classify each study action. Some expose you to information; others ask you to produce or apply it. Both can have a purpose.

Example 1 of 4

03 / TRANSFER TO YOUR OWN STUDIES

Choose your next step.

Take an actual question from your current subject. Explain a choice from memory, contrast another and identify what you still cannot explain. Choose a new question after revisiting that gap.

Local demonstration only. No tracking, uploads, networking or medical advice. Real public/paid release would require subject, rights, design and accessibility review.
